Welcome to DIU Library

The DIU Central Library is for all students of Daffodil International University. Here students can read books, study for exams, and borrow books for home.

Library Time: Saturday to Thursday, 9 AM to 8 PM. Friday Closed.

Address: Daffodil Smart City, Birulia, Savar, Dhaka - 1216

Notice: Please bring your DIU ID Card to enter the library. Books must be returned within 7 days.

About The Library

Our library has books for CSE, EEE, BBA, English and other departments. There is a reading room, computer facility and e-library for students.

Students can search books, read newspapers, and use the internet for study purpose. The library is a quiet place, so everyone must maintain silence.

Facilities:

  • Reading Room
  • Book Borrowing System
  • Computer and WiFi
  • E-books and Journals
  • Newspaper Section

Library Rules

  1. DIU ID Card is mandatory.
  2. Keep silence inside the library.
  3. Return books within 7 days.
  4. Do not write or tear pages.
  5. Keep mobile on silent mode.
  6. Food is not allowed inside.
