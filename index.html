📚 Library Keno Eto Dorkari?

Library sudhu boi rakhar jayga na, eita jibon bodle deoar jayga

🧠

1. Gyan Bare

Boi porle notun notun bishoy jana jay. Porikkhay valo result, chakrir porikkhay egiye thaka - sob boi thekei ase.

💡

2. Chinta Shokti Bare

Boi manusher chinta korar khomota baray. Jara boi pore tara valo siddhanto nite pare.

📱

3. Mobile Theke Mukti

Saradin mobile na dekhe library te 1 ghonta boi porle mon valo thake, chokh valo thake.

🎯

4. Sopno Puron Hoy

Boro boro manush - Kazi Nazrul, Humayun Ahmed sobai library theke boro hoyeche. Tomar sopnoo ekhan theke suru hobe.

🤝

5. Bondhutto Hoy

Library te valo valo bondhu pawa jay, jara porasuna niye vabe.

💰

6. Taka Bachay

Dami dami boi kinar dorkar nai. Library theke FREE te sob boi pora jay.
